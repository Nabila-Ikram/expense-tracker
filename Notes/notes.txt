/*
min-h-0 is important in flex layouts.

By default, flex children have min-height:auto,
which means they cannot shrink smaller than their content.

Example:
If Transactions contains many cards, its content height may become
larger than the available screen height, causing overflow.

min-h-0 allows the flex child to shrink and take only the available
space. Then overflow-y-auto can create a scrollbar inside that area.

Parent controls available height:
h-screen -> full viewport height
Navbar -> h-16 (64px)
Main area -> remaining height using calc()

flex-1 gives the content area the remaining space.
min-h-0 allows it to shrink.
overflow-y-auto handles extra content.
*/
